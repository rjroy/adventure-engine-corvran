# Kael Ashenmoor

## Keywords

### Pyromancer's Fury (+2)
**Origin**: Kael's fire manifested during a childhood trauma when bandits burned his village. The fire answered his rage and has been his weapon ever since.
**Applies When**: Using fire offensively in combat, intimidating through displays of heat or flame, resisting fire or heat, sensing fire-based magic or traps
**Does NOT Apply**: Subtle magic or illusion, healing or protection, diplomacy or persuasion (fire is a weapon, not a tool of reason), controlling fire in precise or delicate ways (Kael's fire is rage, not artistry)

### Survivor of the Ashenmoor Burning (+1)
**Origin**: The destruction of his village and the three years spent wandering as a refugee before finding shelter in the Free City.
**Applies When**: Enduring hardship or deprivation, navigating wilderness without supplies, reading danger in unfamiliar environments, relating to displaced or desperate people
**Does NOT Apply**: Social situations among the wealthy or powerful, scholarly knowledge, any situation requiring trust in institutions or authority, combat (survival is about endurance, not fighting)

### Debt to the Unmarked (+1)
**Origin**: The Unmarked, a secretive group of fire-touched outcasts, took Kael in and taught him to control his flames. He owes them a debt he hasn't fully understood yet.
**Applies When**: Recognizing symbols or coded messages of secretive organizations, navigating underground or criminal networks, keeping secrets under pressure, resisting magical compulsion (the Unmarked taught him mental discipline)
**Does NOT Apply**: Open confrontation or public leadership, knowledge of legitimate political structures, anything requiring social respectability, magic beyond fire (the Unmarked taught discipline, not breadth)

## Notes

### Appearance
Lean and wiry, with burn scars tracing up his forearms and the left side of his neck. Dark hair kept short. Eyes that catch the light like embers. Wears a leather coat too heavy for the weather, hiding the scars.

### Personality
Quiet and watchful. Slow to trust but fiercely loyal once trust is earned. Has a temper he works hard to control, knowing what happens when he doesn't. Pragmatic about violence but haunted by it.

### Goals
Find out what the Unmarked actually want from him. The debt they hold isn't just gratitude; there's something else. He needs to understand it before it comes due.

### Backstory
The village of Ashenmoor burned when Kael was twelve. Bandits lit the fires, but when they cornered Kael in the granary, the fire answered him instead. He doesn't remember what happened next. The bandits didn't survive. Neither did the granary, or the three houses beside it.

Three years on the road taught him to survive. The Unmarked found him trying to burn a meal without burning a forest and offered him control. He spent four years with them, learning discipline, earning trust, accumulating a debt he's only beginning to understand the shape of.
