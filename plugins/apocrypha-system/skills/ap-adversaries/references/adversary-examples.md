# Adversary Examples

One example of each tier demonstrating keyword design, Fear abilities, and stress thresholds.

---

## Desperate Brigand (Minor)

*A ragged highway robber, more hungry than dangerous, armed with a rusty short sword and the courage of nothing left to lose.*

**Difficulty**: 12

**Stress Threshold**: 2 (Aggregate tracking)

### Keywords
- Desperate Cutthroat (+1)
- Knows the Back Roads (+1)

### Fear Abilities

None. Minor adversaries often have no Fear abilities.

### GM Notes

This brigand folds fast. Two stress from a single Success with Hope defeats it. Use brigands in groups (2-4) rather than solo. On failure, the brigand's response is opportunistic: snatching a pack, shoving the character off the road, or fleeing with whatever they grabbed.

**Stress tracking**: Aggregate. Mark stress as a single counter: "Brigand: 1/2 stress."

## Iron Fang, Pack Alpha (Standard)

*A massive wolf with a scarred muzzle and iron-gray fur. Leads a pack of six through coordinated, patient hunts. Intelligent enough to test prey before committing.*

**Difficulty**: 15

**Stress Threshold**: 5 (Per-keyword tracking)

### Keywords
- Pack Commander (+2) [Stress: 0]
- Ironjaw Bite (+2) [Stress: 0]
- Patient Hunter (+1) [Stress: 0]

### Fear Abilities
- **Coordinated Takedown**: The pack lunges as one. Inflict light stress on a movement or agility-related keyword. (Cost: 1 Fear)
- **Retreat and Regroup**: The Alpha withdraws, healing 1 stress from itself. The pack repositions. (Cost: 2 Fear)

### GM Notes

Iron Fang fights smart. It tests the character with its pack first, staying back to observe. "Patient Hunter" applies when stalking, circling, or choosing the moment to engage. "Pack Commander" applies when coordinating the wolves' attacks.

As keywords take stress, narrate the degradation:
- "Pack Commander" at 1 stress: The wolves hesitate, miss their cues, crash into each other.
- "Ironjaw Bite" at 2 stress: The jaw is damaged, teeth broken. Bites are weaker, grip uncertain.

**Stress tracking**: Per-keyword. A Success with Hope deals 2 stress to one keyword. When total stress across all keywords reaches 5, the Alpha is defeated (likely retreats, dragging its wounded pack with it).

**Example fight state after 2 rounds**:

| Keyword | Base | Stress | Effective |
|---------|------|--------|-----------|
| Pack Commander | +2 | 1 | +1 |
| Ironjaw Bite | +2 | 2 | +0 |
| Patient Hunter | +1 | 0 | +1 |

Total stress: 3/5. Two more stress defeats it.

## Vareth the Hollow King (Major)

*Once a just ruler, now a revenant bound to his throne by a curse he cannot break. His crown is fused to his skull. His eyes are empty pits that weep black ichor. He does not want to fight, but the curse does not ask what he wants.*

**Difficulty**: 18

**Stress Threshold**: 10 (Per-keyword tracking)

### Keywords
- Crown of the Unbroken Oath (+3) [Stress: 0]
- Sovereign Command (+3) [Stress: 0]
- Deathless Endurance (+2) [Stress: 0]
- Hollow Grief (+2) [Stress: 0]
- Cursed Blade of the Throne (+2) [Stress: 0]

### Fear Abilities
- **Voice of the Crown**: Vareth commands obedience. Inflict light stress on a willpower, courage, or loyalty-related keyword. (Cost: 1 Fear)
- **The Curse Hungers**: The curse drains life from the surroundings. Inflict deep stress on one keyword. Narrate the character's capability withering, as if the curse is unmaking what makes them who they are. (Cost: 2 Fear)
- **Rise, My Fallen**: Vareth raises 2 minor undead servants (threshold 2, keywords: Undying Loyalty +1, Cold Grip +1). (Cost: 2 Fear)
- **I Will Not Break**: Vareth removes 3 stress from himself. The crown flares with dark light as the curse reasserts its hold. (Cost: 3 Fear)

### GM Notes

Vareth is a tragic fight. The player should feel the weight of fighting someone who doesn't want to be fighting. "Hollow Grief" applies when Vareth hesitates, shows flashes of the person he was, or when the curse visibly forces him to act against his nature.

As keywords take stress, narrate the curse weakening:
- "Crown of the Unbroken Oath" at 2 stress: The crown flickers, and for a moment Vareth's eyes clear. He whispers "please" before the curse reasserts.
- "Sovereign Command" at 3 stress: His commands come broken, contradictory. The curse is losing its grip on his voice.
- "Deathless Endurance" at 2 stress: He staggers, falls to one knee. The revenant body is failing.

**Defeating Vareth** (10 total stress): The curse shatters. Vareth collapses, the crown cracking. Whether he finds peace or becomes something else is a story question, not a mechanical one.

**Fear ability strategy**:
- Open with "Voice of the Crown" to establish Vareth's authority
- Use "The Curse Hungers" at dramatic moments when Fear pool is high
- "Rise, My Fallen" creates tactical pressure and gives the player more targets
- Save "I Will Not Break" for when Vareth is near defeat to extend the fight

**Example fight state at the midpoint**:

| Keyword | Base | Stress | Effective |
|---------|------|--------|-----------|
| Crown of the Unbroken Oath | +3 | 2 | +1 |
| Sovereign Command | +3 | 1 | +2 |
| Deathless Endurance | +2 | 1 | +1 |
| Hollow Grief | +2 | 0 | +2 |
| Cursed Blade of the Throne | +2 | 1 | +1 |

Total stress: 5/10. Halfway there. The fight is turning, but Vareth still has "I Will Not Break" in reserve.
