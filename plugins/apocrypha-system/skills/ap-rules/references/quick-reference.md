# Apocrypha Quick Reference

## Resolution

Roll 2d12 (hope + fear) + keyword modifier vs difficulty.

| Difficulty | Target |
|------------|--------|
| Routine | 10 |
| Moderate | 14 |
| Hard | 17 |
| Desperate | 20 |

## Outcomes

| Result | Tokens | Narrative |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Success + Hope higher | Player gains Hope | Clean success |
| Success + Fear higher | GM gains Fear | Success with complication |
| Failure + Hope higher | Player may gain Hope | Partial failure, gain info/positioning/Hope |
| Failure + Fear higher | GM gains Fear | Hard failure |
| Doubles (critical) | No tokens | Extraordinary success or dramatic failure |

## Stress

| Level | Effect | Clearing |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Light | -1 modifier | Rest or 1 Hope |
| Deep | -2 modifier | Narrative resolution scene |
| Max | -3 (light + deep) | Spread to other keywords |

## Hope (Player, max 6)

- Reroll one die: 1 Hope
- Clear light stress: 1 Hope
- Narrative declaration: 2 Hope

## Fear (GM, max 12)

- Light stress on keyword: 1 Fear
- Deep stress on keyword: 2 Fear
- Complication outside a roll: 1 Fear
- Adversary Fear ability: cost varies
- Scene interrupt: 3 Fear

## Adversary Stress

- Success with Hope: deal 2 stress
- Success with Fear: deal 1 stress
- Defeated when total stress >= threshold

## Combat

Player always rolls. No initiative. Adversary actions come through failure consequences and Fear spending.
