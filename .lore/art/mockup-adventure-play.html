State 1: Mid-conversation (idle, player's turn)
‹ Lost Mines of Phandelver Adventure Engine
Game Master
You push open the heavy oak door and find yourselves in a torchlit chamber. The air smells of damp stone and old leather. Along the far wall, three iron-banded chests sit in a row, each sealed with a different lock — one brass, one iron, one that seems to be fashioned from a black material you don't recognize.

In the center of the room, a stone pedestal holds an open tome, its pages yellowed with age. Faint runes glow along the margins.
You
I approach the pedestal carefully and try to read the runes. Does my character have Arcana proficiency?
Game Master
Thorin Ironforge, you peer at the runes, drawing on your years in the mountain libraries. Let's see how well your Arcana training serves you here.
⚄ Rolled 1d20 + 4 (Arcana) → 16. Success against DC 13.
The runes resolve into meaning: a warning, in old Dwarvish. "Speak the keeper's name or face the warden's fire." Below the warning, a name has been deliberately scratched out — but you can make out the first three letters: Mog.

The tome's pages begin to tremble, as if responding to your gaze.
You
I step back from the pedestal and look at the chest with the black lock more closely. I want to examine it — any markings or mechanisms I can see?
Game Master
You crouch beside the third chest. Up close, the "lock" reveals itself to be no lock at all — it's a sealed mouth, carved in stone, lips pressed tight. No keyhole. No visible mechanism. The black material is obsidian, warm to the touch despite the room's chill.

Something tells you this chest doesn't open with a key.
