## Meeting Notes: Keyword RPG System Spec Review

A comprehensive documentation review of the Keyword RPG System spec identified nine open questions spanning clarity, completeness, consistency, and verifiability. Rather than treating these as defects, the findings were folded directly back into the spec document as inline callouts and a consolidated index. This positions the spec as a working artifact for brainstorming rather than a locked requirement.

The review confirmed the system's core design philosophy is coherent and consistently applied: a 2d6 hope/fear roll generating tokens that mechanically permission the GM to create adversity without prompt judgment calls. Keywords with positive scope and explicit exclusions prevent authority drift. The act-based progression structure is naturally LLM-native. Three critical gaps emerged: (1) no defined combat procedure stating whether adversaries act autonomously or only through GM Fear spending, (2) no mechanical defeat state for player characters at extreme stress, (3) a progression math off-by-one (18 acts from level 1 produces level 19, not 18).

Smaller issues include ambiguity on adversary stress tracking (per-keyword like players or aggregate damage pool), no Fear ability design guidance for different adversary tiers, incorrect Fear token display on player character sheets, and undefined recovery criteria for deep stress. All findings are now embedded in the spec at their relevant requirement locations, with a decision table at the end listing each open question, its impact, and a tentative leaning for the user's consideration.

**No decisions made.** The spec remains in draft status, prepared as a brainstorm artifact. The user indicated confidence in making changes and closed the session.
