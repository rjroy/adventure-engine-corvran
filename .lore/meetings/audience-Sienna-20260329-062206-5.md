Meeting Notes: Corvran Visual Identity & Logo Integration

Sienna and the user reviewed the visual assets for Adventure Engine of Corvran, focusing on logo transparency, SVG replacement in mockups, and color palette standardization. The logo.png was regenerated to remove white padding and maintain transparency. All SVG logo instances across mockup-adventure-list.html and mockup-adventure-play.html (four header instances and two decorative empty-state ravens) were replaced with the PNG at appropriate sizes: 28×28 for headers, 64×64 for decorative use. The amber background wrapper CSS was removed since the PNG renders directly on the dark header background.

The conversation included discussion of color format selection. Hex values had been used throughout due to training prevalence, despite HSL and LCH being superior for design system consistency. The user noted this as a systemic AI bias worth capturing for future work. The mockup HTML files were converted to LCH values by the user, and the corvran-visual-brief.md was audited and corrected. Two key mismatches were identified and resolved: --bg-elevated L value (rounding correction) and --tool-accent (had been copy-pasted from gm-accent, correct value is sage green at lch(60% 30 133)). The brief was updated to reflect the mockups as the canonical source, expanding the palette table from 11 tokens to 21 tokens and correcting values for --bg-surface, --text-secondary, --text-tertiary, and --gm-accent.

All color tokens are now consistently defined in LCH format across mockup-adventure-list.html, mockup-adventure-play.html, and corvran-visual-brief.md. The visual identity is complete and internally consistent for development.
