# The Lost Mines of Phandelver

The frontier town of **Phandalin** sits at the edge of civilization, a day's ride from the ruins of the legendary Wave Echo Cave. Once home to a powerful dwarven mine, the cave was lost centuries ago when orcs swept through the region.

## Key Locations

- **Phandalin:** A rough-and-tumble frontier town with a trading post, inn, and shrine
- **Tresendar Manor:** A ruined estate on the hill above town, rumored to be a hideout for the Redbrands
- **Wave Echo Cave:** The legendary lost mine, somewhere in the hills to the east

## Key NPCs

- **Gundren Rockseeker:** A dwarven prospector who claims to have found the entrance to Wave Echo Cave
- **Sildar Hallwinter:** A human warrior and agent of the Lords' Alliance, traveling with Gundren
- **Halia Thornton:** Runs the Phandalin Miner's Exchange; has her own agenda

## Starting Hook

Gundren Rockseeker has hired you to escort a wagon of supplies from Neverwinter to Phandalin. He rode ahead with Sildar Hallwinter to "take care of business." The pay is 10 gold pieces each, delivered on arrival. The road south is long, and Gundren seemed nervous when he left.
