# Tharivol Brightblade

**Race:** Half-Elf
**Class:** Fighter
**Level:** 1

A wandering sellsword with a quick blade and a quicker tongue. Tharivol came to Phandalin following rumors of lost dwarven mines and the promise of easy coin. He carries a longsword, chain mail, and a healthy skepticism of anything that sounds too good to be true.
